Privacy policy

AI Studio 1990 Uploader · last updated 20 October 2026

Who uses it

This tool has exactly one user: its owner, the creator of the YouTube channel Ai studio 1990. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through Google's own sign-in screen, the tool uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos the owner made to the owner's own YouTube channel;
  • read that channel's basic details and the status of its videos (for example, whether an upload has finished processing).

It cannot edit or delete videos.

What it stores

The sign-in token and a log of the uploads (video file name, title, video ID and date) are kept only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to anyone except Google/YouTube, and only to perform the uploads above.

What it does not do

It does not collect, sell, share or analyse data about any other person. It has no analytics, no advertising and no cookies.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
